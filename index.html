MACRO / RANDOM · CROSS-CONNECTION MASTER BUS

MACRO CROSS-CONNECTIONS

Each macro can affect several pedals at once, including opposing movement. Randomise changes macro positions, not the routing. Cross-connections are control signals, not feedback audio loops.

04 / CONTINUUM MOSAIC — 48-CHANNEL PERFORMANCE MIXER

48 channels · four buses · independent level and pan

48 functional level/pan/mute/solo strips, each assignable to one of four internal buses. The input is replicated to the strips as a prototype signal source; this is not 48 independent hardware audio inputs. The four buses feed the master.
